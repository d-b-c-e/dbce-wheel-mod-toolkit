## What was wrong

MAME's default `winhybrid` provider capped wheels at 32 buttons; glfw-compacted
axes broke sparse pedal sets; centre-resting pedals read 50%; the FFB Arcade
Plugin approach (external `dinput8.dll` hijack) had a fixed effect length that
made forces come and go, per-game ini keys that silently no-op'd strength, and
an SDL GUID match that broke across SDL builds. The displayed speed exists in
no dumped memory.

## What the mod does

- **FFB from the driver** (2026-09-03 pivot, on Endprodukt's advice): SDL2
  haptics loaded at runtime, one constant effect on the steering axis, driven
  by the game's own wheel-motor byte. Measured laws: USA is a **damper** (kick
  opposite to wheel velocity, ~100 ms decay, no static force); World holds
  sustained ±126; Exotica is a centring spring. Slew, peak clamp and a 500 ms
  hold watchdog upstream of output; three-pass device selection that turns FFB
  **off** rather than falling through if the named device cannot do haptics;
  sign measured on hardware (positive byte pushes right; positive SDL level
  turns the R12 left).
- Telemetry: Forza 324 from the driver with **speed by HUD OCR** and RPM from a
  hunted DSP address (r = +0.91); JSON UDP mirror of every output; launcher
  `ForzaKeeper` keeps the dash at zero between games.
- Input: DIJOYSTATE2 128-button patch; buttons 33–48 → `ADDSW`; DirectInput
  axis-slot enumerator; half-axis tokens; settle-then-arm wizard; Raw Input
  >32-button listener; stale-process guard.
- Steering gain/curve patch in `ioport.cpp`; widescreen via margin unclip and
  in-memory patches (FOV patching **does not** avoid the revealed-backdrop
  artefact — documented negative result).
- Support bundle, `ffb_trace_report` oscillation verdict, GitHub-Releases updater.

## Findings that transferred

SDL 2.26 GUID layout change; never hard-kill with FFB live; damper-loop
instability ladder; glfw vs DirectInput axis slots; `winhybrid` ≠ `dinput`;
`keybd_event` never reaches rawinput; GDI capture of GL is black; degenerate
zero Forza packets wedge SimHub; four decimal places.

## Relationship to the toolkit

Seed for the **SDL2 backend**; consumer of the Python tools, shaping constants
and device-quirk data now; possible consumer of the DLL's SDL2 backend later,
if the conformance probe shows no regression. Its diagnostics move into the
toolkit.
